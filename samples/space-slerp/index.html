Space Slerp

Beacons light up around your ship. Click one to turn the nose toward it and match its docking mark before its fuse burns out. The ship's orientation is a quaternion.

Click a beacon: turn to dock with it · click empty space: just point there · Space turn to the most urgent beacon · Drag or arrows: orbit the view · Lenses keys 1–5 · Restart R · Pause P
World: right-handed, y up, nose along −z. Quaternion q = [w, x, y, z], Hamilton product, column vectors.
