Gimbal Plane

Fly through 20 gates before the clock runs out. The plane is steered with three Euler angles, and you can fly it into gimbal lock.

Pitch W / S or ↑ / ↓ (W = nose up) · Yaw A / D or ← / → · Roll Q / E · Lenses keys 1–5 · Restart R · Pause P
World: right-handed, y up, the plane's nose along −z.
